This application ("the App") is provided solely as an educational tool for students and instructors participating in a graduate-level course in clinical informatics at the University of Washington. It is developed and maintained by course instructional staff for classroom and lab use only.

Educational use only

The App is intended exclusively for instructional purposes, including exploring the structure of clinical data, practicing FHIR API interactions, and demonstrating health information exchange concepts. It is not intended for, and must not be used for, any clinical, diagnostic, treatment, billing, or other real-world healthcare operations purpose.

No real patient data

The App connects only to Epic's publicly available non-production ("sandbox") FHIR environment, which contains synthetic test data. No real patient information is created, stored, transmitted, or accessed by this App at any time.

No warranty

The App is provided "as is," for classroom use, without warranty of any kind, express or implied. It is maintained on a best-effort basis by course staff and may be modified, updated, or discontinued at any time without notice.

Data handling

Any data retrieved through the App during a class session is sandbox test data and is not retained by the App beyond the current browser session. No student account credentials, personal information, or real health data are collected or stored by the App itself.

Contact

Questions about this App or its use in coursework should be directed to the course instructor.
